Unified Market Guidance
Run an analysis to load SPY, Nasdaq, Dow, volatility, oil, rates and official macro-event guidance.
The guidance layer uses live/near-live market prices plus official BLS, BEA and Federal Reserve release calendars when reachable. It can confirm, weaken, size down or block a new trade around event risk, but it cannot independently flip Halifax from LONG to SHORT or from SHORT to LONG.
Bond & Rates Risk Dashboard
Run an analysis to load Treasury, duration and credit-risk context.
Bond/rates context is a macro guardrail. It can strengthen or weaken the macro score and trade quality, but it does not independently reverse a price-action LONG into a SHORT or vice versa.
Paper Signal Journal — Local Track Record
Transparent Paper-Trade Record
Use PAPER TRADE after a Halifax LONG or SHORT signal. The journal stores the exact entry, stop, target, score and timestamp in this browser. Closing a paper signal requires you to enter the exit price manually; Halifax then calculates the realized R result. It does not invent fills or reconstruct an unobserved price path.
Historical Strategy Lab — Daily + Prior Weekly
Point-in-Time Halifax Backtest
Use selected benchmark
This replays one of four fixed point-in-time technical strategies using Daily structure plus the prior completed Weekly bar, with optional benchmark relative strength. It intentionally excludes historical news, current macro overrides, smart-money filings, and live intraday confirmation because those data are not reconstructed point-in-time here. Signals use the close; entries use the next session open. Slippage, ATR/structure stops, stop caps, and non-overlapping trades are applied.
Choose settings and run the historical backtest.
